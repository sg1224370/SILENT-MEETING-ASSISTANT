🛠️ How to Start the App in the Future
1. Start the Backend:
powershell

cd "d:\project\hand-gesture-recognition-using-mediapipe-main - Copy\hand-gesture-recognition-using-mediapipe-main"

powershell

Navigate to http://localhost:5173/ in your browser, grant webcam access, and show gestures to your camera!
